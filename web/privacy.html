2. Hvilke oplysninger, og hvorfor?

Når du booker en service

Vi behandler dit navn, din adresse, dit telefonnummer og din e-mail, de services du vælger, tidspunktet, din cykeltype, din besked til os, eventuelle billeder du vedhæfter, prisen, og at du har accepteret handelsbetingelserne og bedt os udføre arbejdet inden for fortrydelsesfristen. Vi bruger oplysningerne til at planlægge og udføre besøget, kontakte dig om bookingen og sende faktura.

Retsgrundlag: Behandlingen er nødvendig for at opfylde aftalen med dig (databeskyttelsesforordningens artikel 6, stk. 1, litra b). Oplysninger, der indgår i vores regnskab, gemmer vi, fordi bogføringsloven kræver det (artikel 6, stk. 1, litra c).

Billeder

Du kan vedhæfte op til 3 billeder af din cykel eller problemet. Billederne bliver gjort mindre i din browser, før de sendes, og det fjerner også oplysninger om, hvor billedet er taget (GPS). Undgå venligst billeder, hvor andre personer kan genkendes.

For at kunne modtage billeder fra besøgende uden en konto, logger siden dig ind anonymt i baggrunden. Det anonyme login indeholder ingen oplysninger om dig; det bruges kun til at sikre, at kun du kan lægge billeder i din egen mappe.

Når du opretter en konto

En konto er frivillig. Opretter du en, gemmer vi dit navn, din e-mail, dit telefonnummer og din adresse, så de er udfyldt, når du booker, og så du kan se og aflyse dine bookinger. Login håndteres af Google (Firebase Authentication), og din adgangskode gemmes kun i en form, der ikke kan læses tilbage; vi kender den ikke. Når du har bekræftet din e-mail, knytter vi de bookinger til kontoen, som du tidligere har lavet med den samme e-mail.

På kontoen kan du gemme oplysninger om dine cykler (fx model, størrelse og bremser) med op til 4 billeder pr. cykel. Efter et besøg gemmer vi en servicehistorik: hvad vi lavede, brugte dele, priser, mekanikerens noter, billeder fra besøget og eventuelle anbefalinger til næste gang. Du kan se det hele på din konto.

Retsgrundlag: Behandlingen er nødvendig for at levere kontoen og vores service til dig (artikel 6, stk. 1, litra b).

Når en virksomhed beder om et møde

Vi behandler kontaktpersonens navn, e-mail og telefon, virksomhedens navn og type, antallet af cykler, det ønskede tidspunkt og beskeden. Retsgrundlag: vores legitime interesse i at besvare henvendelsen og indgå en aftale med virksomheden (artikel 6, stk. 1, litra f).

Virksomhedskunder

Har en virksomhed en aftale med os, opretter vi et login til virksomhedens kontaktperson. Vi gemmer kontaktpersonens navn, e-mail og telefon, virksomhedens navn og adresse, aftalen, oplysninger om flådens cykler (nummer, type, placering og billeder, som vi tager ved eftersynet) og de månedlige rapporter. Rapporterne handler om cyklerne, ikke om de personer, der bruger dem. Retsgrundlag: vores legitime interesse i at opfylde aftalen med virksomheden (artikel 6, stk. 1, litra f).

Sikkerhed og drift

Vores leverandør registrerer tekniske oplysninger som IP-adresse og tidspunkt, når siden bruges, for at holde tjenesten sikker og stabil (artikel 6, stk. 1, litra f).

3. Hvem får oplysningerne?

  • Google (Firebase) driver hjemmesiden og login og opbevarer bookinger, konti og billeder for os som databehandler. Data opbevares i EU (Belgien). Google er en amerikansk virksomhed; eventuel overførsel til USA sker på grundlag af EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework og EU's standardkontraktbestemmelser i Googles databehandleraftale.
  • Adresseforslag: mens du skriver din adresse, sender vores server det, du har skrevet, til Klimadatastyrelsens Adressevælger (Danmarks officielle adresseregister) for at finde forslag. Der sendes kun adresseteksten, ikke dit navn eller andre oplysninger.
  • E-mail: bekræftelser og beskeder sendes via [e-mailudbyder].
  • Regnskab: [revisor/bogholder, hvis relevant] får de oplysninger, der står på fakturaen.

Vi sælger aldrig dine oplysninger og bruger dem ikke til markedsføring uden dit samtykke.

4. Hvor længe gemmer vi dem?

  • Bookinger: [3 år] efter besøget. Oplysninger, der indgår i regnskabet (fx fakturaer), gemmes i 5 år fra udgangen af det regnskabsår, de vedrører, som bogføringsloven kræver.
  • Billeder: gemmes som en del af din cykels servicehistorik, indtil vi sletter dem, eller du beder os om det. Når et billede slettes, er det helt væk inden for 7 dage.

5. Oplysninger i din browser

Vi bruger ikke cookies til statistik eller markedsføring. Siden gemmer kun det, der er nødvendigt, i din egen browser:

  • dit valg af sprog (dansk eller engelsk),
  • dine kontaktoplysninger, hvis du sætter flueben ved Husk mig, så formularen er udfyldt næste gang. Fjern fluebenet ved næste booking for at slette dem,
  • det anonyme login, når du vedhæfter billeder,
  • dit login, når du er logget ind på din konto, så du ikke skal logge ind hver gang. Log ud for at fjerne det.

Skriv til os, så svarer vi inden for en måned.

2. What information, and why?

When you book a service

We process your name, address, phone number and email, the services you choose, the time, your type of bike, your message to us, any photos you attach, the price, and that you accepted the terms and asked us to do the work within the withdrawal period. We use them to plan and carry out the visit, contact you about the booking and send the invoice.

Legal basis: processing is necessary to perform our agreement with you (Article 6(1)(b) of the General Data Protection Regulation). Information that forms part of our accounts is kept because the Danish Bookkeeping Act requires it (Article 6(1)(c)).

Photos

You can attach up to 3 photos of your bike or the problem. They are made smaller in your browser before they are sent, which also removes information about where the photo was taken (GPS). Please avoid photos in which other people can be recognised.

To accept photos from visitors without an account, the site signs you in anonymously in the background. The anonymous sign-in holds no information about you; it only makes sure that only you can put photos in your own folder.

When you create an account

An account is optional. If you create one, we keep your name, email, phone number and address, so they are filled in when you book and you can see and cancel your bookings. Sign-in is handled by Google (Firebase Authentication), and your password is stored only in a form that cannot be read back; we do not know it. Once you have confirmed your email, we add the bookings you made earlier with the same email to your account.

In your account you can save details of your bikes (such as model, size and brakes), with up to 4 photos per bike. After a visit we keep a service history: what we did, parts used, prices, the mechanic's notes, photos from the visit and any recommendations for next time. You can see all of it in your account.

Legal basis: processing is necessary to provide the account and our service to you (Article 6(1)(b)).

When a business asks for a meeting

We process the contact person's name, email and phone, the business's name and type, the number of bikes, the preferred time and the message. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in answering the enquiry and entering into an agreement with the business (Article 6(1)(f)).

Business customers

When a business has an agreement with us, we create a login for its contact person. We keep the contact person's name, email and phone, the business's name and address, the agreement, details of the fleet's bikes (number, type, location and photos we take at the check-up) and the monthly reports. The reports are about the bikes, not about the people who use them. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in performing the agreement with the business (Article 6(1)(f)).

Security and operation

Our provider records technical information such as IP address and time when the site is used, to keep the service secure and working (Article 6(1)(f)).

3. Who receives the information?

  • Google (Firebase) runs the website and sign-in and stores bookings, accounts and photos for us as a data processor. Data is stored in the EU (Belgium). Google is a US company; any transfer to the US is based on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and the EU standard contractual clauses in Google's data processing terms.
  • Address suggestions: while you type your address, our server sends what you have typed to Klimadatastyrelsen's Adressevælger (Denmark's official address register) to find suggestions. Only the address text is sent, not your name or anything else.
  • Email: confirmations and messages are sent through [email provider].
  • Accounts: [accountant or bookkeeper, if any] receives the information on the invoice.

We never sell your information, and we do not use it for marketing without your consent.

4. How long do we keep it?

  • Bookings: [3 years] after the visit. Information that forms part of the accounts (such as invoices) is kept for 5 years from the end of the financial year it relates to, as the Bookkeeping Act requires.
  • Photos: kept as part of your bike's service history until we delete them or you ask us to. Once a photo is deleted, it is completely gone within 7 days.
  • Photos uploaded without completing the booking: deleted automatically after about a day.
  • Enquiries from businesses: [12 months] if no agreement is made.
  • Your account: for as long as you have it. Write to us if you want it deleted; we then delete your profile, your saved bikes and their photos. Bookings and service history are kept as described under Bookings.
  • Business agreements: while the agreement runs and [3 years] after it ends. Accounting information is kept for 5 years, as above.

5. Information in your browser

We do not use cookies for statistics or marketing. The site keeps only what is needed, in your own browser:

  • your choice of language (Danish or English),
  • your contact details, if you tick Remember me, so the form is filled in next time. Untick it at your next booking to delete them,
  • the anonymous sign-in, when you attach photos,
  • your sign-in, when you are logged in to your account, so you do not have to log in every time. Log out to remove it.

6. Your rights

You have the right to see the information we hold about you, have incorrect information corrected, have information deleted, have processing restricted, receive your information in a portable form, and object to processing based on our legitimate interest. Write to us and we will reply within one month.

Some information we must keep, for example because the Bookkeeping Act requires it.

You can complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet), datatilsynet.dk, if you are unhappy with how we handle your information.
