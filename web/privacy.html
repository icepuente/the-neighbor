2. Hjemmesiden

Hjemmesiden har ingen konti, ingen cookies, ingen statistik og intet indhold fra andre tjenester. Det, du skriver i bookingformularen, bliver i din egen browser; siden sender det ikke nogen steder hen. Når du trykker på knappen for at sende, åbner dit eget mailprogram med teksten, og du bestemmer selv, om du vil sende den.

Siden gemmer kun dit valg af sprog (dansk eller engelsk) i din egen browser.

Siden leveres gennem Cloudflare, som behandler tekniske oplysninger som din IP-adresse for at vise siden og beskytte den mod angreb. Vores egen server fører ikke log over besøgende.

3. Når du sender os en forespørgsel

Vi modtager det, der står i din mail: dit navn, din adresse, dit telefonnummer, den e-mailadresse, du skriver fra, de reparationer og det tidspunkt, du ønsker, din cykeltype, din besked og eventuelle billeder, du selv vedhæfter. Vi bruger oplysningerne til at svare dig, udføre reparationen og sende faktura.

Retsgrundlag: Behandlingen er nødvendig for at indgå og opfylde aftalen med dig (databeskyttelsesforordningens artikel 6, stk. 1, litra b). Fakturaer gemmer vi, fordi bogføringsloven kræver det (artikel 6, stk. 1, litra c).

6. Dine rettigheder

Du har ret til at få indsigt i de oplysninger, vi har om dig, få forkerte oplysninger rettet, få oplysninger slettet, få behandlingen begrænset og få dine oplysninger udleveret. Skriv til os, så svarer vi inden for en måned. Fakturaer skal vi dog gemme, som bogføringsloven kræver.

Du kan klage til Datatilsynet, datatilsynet.dk, hvis du er utilfreds med vores behandling af dine oplysninger.

2. The website

The website has no accounts, no cookies, no statistics and no content from other services. What you type in the booking form stays in your own browser; the site does not send it anywhere. When you press the button to send, your own mail program opens with the text, and you decide whether to send it.

The site only keeps your choice of language (Danish or English), in your own browser.

The site is delivered through Cloudflare, which processes technical information such as your IP address to show the site and protect it from attacks. Our own server keeps no log of visitors.

3. When you send us a request

We receive what is in your email: your name, address and phone number, the email address you write from, the repairs and time you would like, your type of bike, your message and any photos you attach yourself. We use this to reply to you, carry out the repair and send the invoice.

Legal basis: processing is necessary to enter into and perform our agreement with you (Article 6(1)(b) of the General Data Protection Regulation). We keep invoices because the Danish Bookkeeping Act requires it (Article 6(1)(c)).

4. Who receives the information?

  • Our email provider [name of email provider] stores our email.
  • Accounts: [accountant or bookkeeper, if any] receives the information on the invoice.

We never sell your information, and we do not use it for marketing.

5. How long do we keep it?

  • Emails about a booking: deleted [12 months] after the visit.
  • Invoices: 5 years from the end of the financial year they relate to, as the Bookkeeping Act requires.

6. Your rights

You have the right to see the information we hold about you, have incorrect information corrected, have information deleted, have processing restricted and receive your information. Write to us and we will reply within one month. Invoices, however, we must keep, as the Bookkeeping Act requires.

You can complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet), datatilsynet.dk, if you are unhappy with how we handle your information.
