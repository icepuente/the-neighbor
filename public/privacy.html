Privacy notice

{{businessName}} collects only what we need to carry out your bike service. This notice explains what we store and why.

What we collect

When you book, we store your name, email address, phone number, the service address, details about your bike, and any notes or messages you send us. We also log the IP address the booking came from, to help prevent abuse.

Why we use it

We use your details to plan and carry out the service, to contact you about your booking (for example confirmations, arrival times and questions), and to keep records for accounting. We do not sell your data or use it for marketing.

Who can see it

Only {{businessName}}. Booking data is stored on our own server in Denmark. Emails are sent through our email provider.

How long we keep it

We keep booking records for as long as needed for the service and for bookkeeping requirements. After that, we delete them.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time by emailing {{contactEmail}} or replying to any of our emails. You can also complain to Datatilsynet, the Danish Data Protection Agency.
