    About

    You don't need a workshop. You need a neighbor who knows bikes.

    The owner does every job personally, with the tools in the van.

    Are parts included?

    No. Parts and bigger repairs are extra, and we always ask before doing them.

    Do you come to us?

    Yes. All work is done at your location.

    Is there a minimum number of bikes?

    No. Premium is for fleets of up to 35 bikes.

    What's in preventive maintenance?

    Brakes, gears, tyre pressure, chain, safety check and basic adjustments.

    Can I see my service history?

    Yes. Your account shows bookings, history, your bikes and our recommendations.
